WHAT WE DO

Our Programs

Practical initiatives focused on awareness, support, education and neurological health — designed to meet people where diagnosis, stigma or isolation would otherwise stop them.

Community Support

Building safe spaces where people affected by epilepsy can connect, share experiences and find support.

Seizure First Aid Training

Hands-on training for families, teachers and first responders on how to respond safely and calmly when a seizure happens.

School & Workplace Outreach

Helping schools and employers understand epilepsy so students and employees are protected, not excluded.

Research & Knowledge Collaboration

Promoting research, knowledge sharing and collaboration around epilepsy and neurological health.

Education & Awareness

Helping families, schools, professionals and communities understand epilepsy and respond with confidence.

Family & Caregiver Support

Guidance and peer connection for the families and caregivers who carry the day-to-day realities of epilepsy alongside their loved ones.

OUR PROCESS

How we work.

Every BSG program follows the same cycle, whether it's a single community workshop or a multi-month campaign.

Identify & Listen

We start by listening to the community — patients, families and health workers — to understand where gaps in knowledge or support exist.

Educate & Train

We deliver accurate, practical information through workshops, training sessions and accessible materials.

Support & Connect

We connect people to peer support, resources and, where possible, the medical care they need.

Measure & Improve

We track what's working and refine our programs so every dollar and every hour goes further.
