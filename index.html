WHY THIS MATTERS

Epilepsy is a global reality.

Behind every statistic is a person, a family and a community. These numbers are why Beyond Seizure Global exists.

50M+

People worldwide live with epilepsy, making it one of the most common neurological conditions on earth.

80%

Of people with epilepsy live in low- and middle-income countries — places like Kenya, where BSG works.

70%

Could live seizure-free with an accurate diagnosis and consistent access to treatment.

3×

Higher risk of premature death for people with epilepsy compared to the general population.

Source: World Health Organization, Epilepsy Fact Sheet (2024) — who.int

OUR APPROACH

Educate. Empower. End Stigma.

Three commitments guide every program, campaign and partnership we take on.

Educate

Plain, accurate information about epilepsy for families, schools and communities.

Empower

Programs and peer support that help people living with epilepsy speak up and be heard.

End Stigma

Awareness campaigns and research that push back on silence, fear and misinformation.
