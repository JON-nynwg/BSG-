HOW WE LEAD

One mission, three commitments

Educate

Plain, accurate information about epilepsy for families, schools and communities.

Empower

Programs and peer support that help people living with epilepsy speak up and be heard.

End Stigma

Awareness campaigns and research that push back on silence, fear and misinformation.
