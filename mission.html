WHY WE EXIST

More than seizures.
A life beyond stigma.

Beyond Seizure Global exists because epilepsy is treatable, manageable and survivable — yet millions of people are still held back by silence, misinformation and fear rather than the condition itself. Our mission is to close that gap.

Educate

Making reliable epilepsy and neurological health information accessible to communities.

Empower

Equipping people affected by epilepsy with knowledge, confidence and meaningful support.

End Stigma

Challenging misconceptions and creating communities where people can live without discrimination.

50M+

people worldwide live with epilepsy, and up to 70% could be seizure-free with proper diagnosis and treatment. That gap between what's possible and what's happening is why BSG exists.

HOW WE GET THERE

Our approach.

Four pillars carry every program BSG runs, from a first-aid workshop in a single classroom to a research partnership that shapes national policy.

01

Awareness & Education

Clear, accurate information about epilepsy delivered where people already are — schools, clinics, churches, workplaces and homes.

02

Community & Direct Support

Support groups, peer connection and family guidance that carry people through diagnosis, treatment and daily life.

03

Research & Evidence

Backing every campaign and program with evidence, so our work reflects what genuinely improves outcomes.

04

Advocacy & Partnerships

Working alongside health professionals, institutions and global frameworks to push for better policy and access to care.

WHAT GUIDES US

Our values.

Compassion

We meet every person where they are, without judgment.

Evidence

We ground our work in credible, up-to-date medical knowledge.

Dignity

People with epilepsy deserve respect — never pity or exclusion.

Partnership

Lasting change is built together — with families, professionals and communities.
