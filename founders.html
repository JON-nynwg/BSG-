THE PEOPLE BEHIND BSG

Two founders.
One conviction.

Beyond Seizure Global was built on a simple, stubborn belief: that no one should have to face epilepsy alone. John and Lilian brought together lived commitment, community insight and research discipline to turn that belief into a working organization — one that educates, empowers and pushes back against stigma every single day.

John N. Mwenga

Founder & Epilepsy Awareness Advocate

John founded Beyond Seizure Global to close the gap between diagnosis and lasting support, leading the organization's awareness campaigns and community outreach for people living with epilepsy.

“We believe no one should face epilepsy alone. Through awareness, compassion, and research, we can break the silence and build a future where every life is understood and supported.”

Lilian Ngina

Co-Founder & Research, Policy and program specialist

Lilian brings research leadership, evidence-based strategy, policy insight, and program innovation to advance BSG's mission and impact connecting frontline awareness.

“When people living with epilepsy feel unable to speak openly about their condition, awareness remains limited. This silence can make it harder to provide the support patients need and to secure the funding essential for life-changing research.”
WHY BSG EXISTS

Awareness first. Everything else follows.

John and Lilian saw the same pattern from two different vantage points — one from community outreach, one from research and policy: silence around epilepsy holds people back as much as seizures themselves. When people can't speak openly about their condition, they struggle to get support, and the research and funding that could change their lives struggles to follow. Beyond Seizure Global exists to break that silence — pairing grassroots awareness with evidence-based programs so that education, empowerment and dignity reach people wherever epilepsy has kept them isolated.
